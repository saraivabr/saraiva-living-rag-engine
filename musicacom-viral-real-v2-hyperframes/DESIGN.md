# Visual identity

Use the official musicacom.ia visual system.

## Colors

- Pulso: `#04E688`
- Pulso profundo: `#02DB79`
- Ônix: `#151923`
- Branco quente: `#F8FBF9`
- Menta: `#A1F4D6`

## Typography

- Headlines: Archivo Black
- Utility copy: IBM Plex Mono

## Direction

The footage is the proof. Presenter and real product screens stay dominant. Graphics behave as annotations: compact, high-contrast, and editorial.

## What not to do

- Do not replace real footage with illustrated fake interfaces.
- Do not cover the presenter's face.
- Do not use generic blue-purple AI gradients.
- Do not keep large text on screen after its beat.
- Do not obscure the real musicacom.ia workflow.
